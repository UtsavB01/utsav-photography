Nature photographer · Ecologist

Utsav Biswas

I study how dryland and wetland ecosystems hold their structure, and what it looks like when they start to lose it — vegetation patterns, tipping points, the slow signs that come before a landscape changes state. The photographs here come from the same fieldwork: birds, mammals, insects and landscapes encountered while collecting data, not staged separately from it.

How this archive works

Photographs are added as they're made, and older frames are added once they've been properly gone back through. Each one is meant to carry enough information — species, place, date, and a short note — that it's useful on its own, not just as an image.
