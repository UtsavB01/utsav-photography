View the portfolio About the work

Two ways of looking at the same place

Most of these photographs were taken in the course of fieldwork — waiting out a survey transect, walking back to camp at dusk, or simply looking up from a dataset for long enough to notice something. The camera and the research question are usually pointed at the same thing: how a landscape holds together, and what happens when it starts to come apart.

This is a working archive rather than a finished body of work. New images are added as they're made — in the field this season, and going back through years of frames not yet looked at properly.
